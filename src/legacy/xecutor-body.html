Simple answers to the questions decorator and production teams usually ask before bringing Xecutor onto the floor.

Ask us something specific →
What is Xecutor?

Xecutor is Swishtag's manufacturing execution platform for decorator and print-on-demand production. It connects orders, artwork, workflows, workstations, quality and fulfillment in one operational system.

Can different products follow different production workflows?

Yes. Workflows can be built around the production path required by the product, including different workstations and stages for methods such as embroidery, screen printing, heat press and other decorator processes.

How do operators use Xecutor on the production floor?

Operators select their workstation, scan the batch QR code, see the job details and instructions, then complete the assigned work so the job can move to the next stage.

Can clients connect Shopify or WooCommerce?

The current Xecutor setup guide includes Shopify app and WooCommerce plugin connectivity for client store setup and order synchronization.

Which systems can Xecutor connect with?

The current setup flow includes Dropbox, Stripe, Mailgun and ShipStation on the administration side, plus Shopify and WooCommerce for connected client stores.

Can Xecutor be adapted to our factory workflow?

Yes. The platform is built around configurable workstations, workflows, product settings, team roles and client setup so the production structure can match the operation.
