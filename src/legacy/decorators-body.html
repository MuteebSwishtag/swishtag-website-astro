Where Production Breaks Down

The floor needs clarity, not another dashboard.

Decorator teams lose time when artwork, production status, job details, and fulfillment live in separate places. The problem is not a lack of software. It is a lack of connected context.

Artwork is separated from the job.

Teams waste time confirming which file or revision is actually approved for production.

Blocked work becomes visible too late.

Missing approval, stock, artwork, or information can hold up production without clear visibility.

Workstations lack production context.

Operators should know exactly what to produce, which file to use, and what happens next.

Completion does not always flow into fulfillment.

Packing, shipping, and status updates can still depend on manual follow up after production ends.
