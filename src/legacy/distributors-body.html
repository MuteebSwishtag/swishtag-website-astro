Distributor reality

The storefront is only one part of the job.

A distributor is coordinating client programs, suppliers, catalog data, approvals, order handoffs, fulfillment models, and internal systems. The technology has to respect that reality.

01
Every client store is slightly different.

POD, stocked inventory, allowances, restricted catalogs, approvals, locations, and program rules change from client to client.

Client programs
02
Product data already lives somewhere.

The goal should be to reuse dependable supplier data where possible, not manually recreate the same catalog information.

Catalogs
03
Orders still have to move through the real operation.

A beautiful storefront is not enough if order routing, supplier handoff, approval, and reporting still depend on manual bridging.

Orders
04
What works for five stores breaks at fifty.

As the portfolio grows, teams need centralized visibility, clearer ownership, and automation around repeated administration.
